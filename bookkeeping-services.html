Bookkeeping

Bookkeeping Services for Growing Businesses

Clean, current books, reconciled every month. The foundation every tax return, loan application and business decision is built on.

What our bookkeeping covers

  • A monthly close, with every account reconciled
  • Every transaction coded to the right vendor and category
  • Accounts payable and accounts receivable
  • A monthly profit and loss statement and balance sheet
  • Personal spending kept separate from business

Why monthly bookkeeping matters

When your books are closed and reconciled every month, the numbers are real on any given day: what came in, what went out and what you actually made. Books stitched together in the spring can only tell you what already happened, when it is too late to act on it.

Clean books also make everything else easier, from tax planning and lender requests to knowing whether you can afford your next hire. Our guide to clean books explains the monthly close and the reports worth reading.

Behind on your books?

If your bookkeeping has fallen behind, we start with a one-time catch-up, scoped to your file. Prior-year cleanup is quoted per year. From there, you move onto a steady monthly rhythm.

How we work

Onboarding starts with a kickoff and secure access in week one, followed by cleanup and your first monthly close in weeks two to four. Our standard is simple: nothing is filed until it has been prepared, independently reviewed and signed off by a partner.

Monthly bookkeeping and reconciliations are included in every level of Real Deal Monthly, our flat-fee membership.

Questions

Bookkeeping questions, answered

How often are my accounts reconciled?

Every month. Your accounts are reconciled and your books are closed monthly, so the numbers are current whenever you need them.

Can you catch up books that are months or years behind?

Yes. Catch-up work is a one-time project scoped to your file, and prior-year cleanup is quoted per year.

Will I get financial statements?

Yes. You receive a monthly profit and loss statement and balance sheet.

Is bookkeeping part of Real Deal Monthly?

Yes. Monthly bookkeeping and reconciliations are included in every Real Deal Monthly membership level.
