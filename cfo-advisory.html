CFO advisory

Fractional CFO and Advisory Services

Executive-level financial leadership and forecasting, without a full-time hire.

What a fractional CFO does

A fractional (or outsourced) CFO provides the strategic side of finance, such as forecasting, cash planning and reporting that leadership can act on, on an ongoing part-time basis instead of through a full-time executive hire. The goal is not a prettier report. It is better decisions.

What our CFO advisory includes

  • Forecasting and budgeting
  • Cash-flow strategy
  • KPI dashboards and a CFO dashboard of the numbers that matter
  • Board-ready reporting
  • Monthly reviews and a standing review call
  • Industry benchmarks
  • Scenario modeling and decision support

Built on clean books

Advisory is only as good as the numbers underneath it, which is why it sits on top of monthly bookkeeping and reporting. Our cash flow and profitability guide covers the margins and break-even math we review with owners.

Questions

CFO advisory questions, answered

Do I need a full-time CFO?

Many growing businesses need CFO-level insight before they need a full-time CFO. Fractional advisory gives you forecasting, cash-flow planning and reporting without hiring a full-time executive.

What reports will I get?

A monthly profit and loss statement and balance sheet, a CFO dashboard of the numbers that matter, and a standing monthly review to talk through what to do next.

How is CFO advisory priced?

The CFO dashboard and monthly review are included in the Advisory and Private levels of Real Deal Monthly, which is one flat monthly fee.
